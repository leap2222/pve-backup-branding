pve-backup

pve-backup is a private, single-user tool that copies encrypted backups from a home Proxmox VE server to the owner's own Google Drive account.

It is not a public service and has no other users. It only accesses the Drive account of its owner, only creates files produced by the owner's server, and does not collect, share or sell any data.

The backups are encrypted on the server before they are uploaded, so Google Drive only stores ciphertext. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
